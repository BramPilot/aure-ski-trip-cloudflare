NO SURPRISES

Frequently asked.
Clearly answered.

If a detail is still TBA, it will be emailed to the address used for registration.

Can beginners join?
Absolutely. Beginners are very welcome. Please note that ski lessons cannot be booked through Sunweb for this short trip. You can explore independent options through ESF.
What exactly is included in the €543 price?
Return group coach transport between the Netherlands and Val Thorens, two nights at Résidence Les Temples du Soleil, a 3-day Val Thorens ski pass and tourist tax.
Can I arrange my own transport?
Yes. You may arrange your own transport, but the bundled coach fare will not be deducted from the €543 trip price. You must inform the Ski Committee at aurelius@hotelschool.nl so Sunweb can be notified.
Where does the coach depart?
The coach departs from The Hague, Schedeldoekshaven 600–592, 2595 AA, at the bus platform by The Hague Central Station, FlixBus stop. Exact departure and arrival times are TBA and will be emailed to participants.
What is not included?
Ski or snowboard equipment, travel and cancellation insurance, meals and drinks, and the optional Les Trois Vallées ski-pass upgrade are not included.
How do registration and payment work?
Register through the official participant link and activate My Sunweb through your confirmation email. A 40% deposit is due within 10 days; the remaining balance must be paid no later than 8 weeks before departure. Pay only through your personal Sunweb payment page — never by bank transfer.
Can I cancel after registering?
Your booking is final as soon as you complete registration, even if you have not yet paid the deposit. Cancellation costs are 40% of the basic trip price up to 57 days before departure; 60% from 56 to 21 days; 80% from 20 to 7 days; and 100% from 6 days before departure through departure day. Within 2 weeks of departure, the percentage is calculated over the full trip price including extras. Cancellation must be emailed to groepsreizen@totallysnow.nl and cannot be reversed. Read the general terms and conditions before confirming.
How does the room allocation work?
There is one accommodation type. If you want to share with a group of up to four, everyone should enter the exact same room name during booking. It remains a preference and the Ski Committee may still adjust the final allocation.
Can I bring or rent equipment?
You may bring your own equipment or add rental equipment during the Sunweb booking process. Current options and prices are shown there. A limited number of travellers may bring their own equipment without extra transport charges. Because coach space is limited, tell the Ski Committee if you plan to bring your own skis or snowboard.
Can I upgrade the ski pass?
Yes. The Les Trois Vallées upgrade costs €76. We recommend keeping the included Val Thorens pass so we can stay together in one ski area, but the choice is yours.
Are ski lessons available?
Not through Sunweb for this trip. If you want to arrange a lesson independently, visit ESF and check availability directly.
Are meals included?
No. Food and drinks are not included in the €543 trip price.
How much luggage can I bring?
Per person: one suitcase or travel bag up to 20 kg and 80 × 70 × 30 cm; one piece of hand luggage up to 2 kg; one pair of skis with poles or one snowboard; and one pair of ski or snowboard boots. Snowboard bindings must be removed. A snowboard bag may contain only one board — no boots, clothing or other items. Food packages are not allowed, all luggage must be labelled, and excess baggage may be refused by the driver. You remain responsible for your belongings. See the Totally Snow coach conditions.
What should I expect on arrival and departure day?
Totally Snow representatives will welcome the group in Val Thorens. Rooms are normally available later in the afternoon; luggage can be stored in the coach luggage area in the morning. Ski passes are collected in advance and the equipment rental partner is informed of the group’s arrival. On departure day, rooms are usually vacated around 08:00 and must be left clean to avoid deposit deductions. Exact check-in, check-out and coach departure times will be confirmed locally.
Is a helmet mandatory?
No, but the Ski Committee strongly recommends wearing one.
Which insurance do I need, and what happens if I am injured?
Travel and cancellation insurance are not included. Check the Sunweb information and the general terms and conditions, and make sure your policy includes winter sports cover.
What are the rules around alcohol?
NIX18 applies: no alcohol under the age of 18. Everyone is expected to act responsibly and follow Aurelius, accommodation and local rules.
Where can I see who has registered?
After registering through Sunweb, participant information remains available through the Aurelius website under Events. There is no separate participant portal on this page.
What happens on the first school day after our return?
Monday 8 February is the first day of Block C. The committee is exploring the options with school, but every participant remains responsible for checking and managing their own academic schedule before booking.
When do we receive departure details and the programme?
The pickup location is confirmed as The Hague Central Station, Schedeldoekshaven 600–592. Exact departure and arrival times, local deposit information, emergency details and the final programme are still TBA. They will be emailed to the address used during registration.
